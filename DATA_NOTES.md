# DATA_NOTES.md — fire-safety-bd

Facts used in this app, where they came from, and what is labelled as
guidance rather than fact. Verified 2026-11-12.

## Verified facts (with sources)

### Emergency numbers
- **999** — National Emergency Service (police, fire service, ambulance),
  free, 24 hours, from any phone. Source: Bangladesh National Portal /
  The Daily Star (launch reporting) and mediabangladesh.net emergency list,
  last verified 28 July 2026.
- **102** — Fire Service & Civil Defence hotline. Launched 2024 to replace
  the 5-digit hotline 16163 (retired 31 Dec 2024); the 11-digit control-room
  number 02223355555 stays active. Sources: The Daily Star, "102, the new
  hotline of fire service"; UNB, "Fire service launches new hotline number
  102"; Bangladesh Pratidin (Nov 2025) also directs the public to 102.
- **02223355555** — FSCD Central Control Room (Dhaka). Source: Observer BD,
  police advisory 14 Oct 2026, which lists this as the Fire Service control
  room number.

### 2025 fire statistics (FSCD press release, Feb 2026)
Reported by BSS (10 Feb 2026), TBS (10 Feb 2026), The Daily Star
(10 Feb 2026) and Daily Sun / Observer BD from the same FSCD release:
- 27,059 fires in 2025 — about 75 per day; 85 killed, 267 injured.
- Property lost ~৳570 crore; property saved ~৳3,263 crore.
- Causes: electrical faults 9,392 (34.71%); bidi/cigarette stubs 4,269
  (15.78%); stoves 2,909 (10.75%); gas cylinder leaks 920 (3.40%);
  gas supply line leaks 562 (2.08%).
- Residential buildings most affected: 8,705 fires (32.17%).
- Dhaka Division recorded the most incidents (7,179).
- March is the peak month in each of the last three years
  (3,522 in Mar 2025; 3,421 in Mar 2024; 3,334 in Mar 2023).
- Cross-check: The Daily Star (6 Jan 2026) gives 2024 = 26,659 fires with
  9,069 (34%) from short circuits, consistent with the same series.

## General safety guidance (standard, not Bangladesh-specific data)
- Extinguisher classes (A solid combustibles, B flammable liquids,
  C gases, E electrical, F cooking oil) and the PASS technique
  (Pull, Aim, Squeeze, Sweep) — standard fire-safety teaching.
- "Never water on oil or electrical fires", "Stop, Drop and Roll",
  "stay low in smoke", "never use a lift in a fire", "close the door,
  block gaps with wet cloth, signal from the window" — standard
  fire-service guidance, also taught by FSCD public-awareness material.
- The risk-check weights (12/10/8) are the app's own editorial scoring,
  not an official scale — labelled in the app as an awareness estimate.

## What this app does NOT claim
- It is not an official Fire Service & Civil Defence product.
- The risk score is not a professional assessment.
- No live data: 2025 is the latest published year at build time.
